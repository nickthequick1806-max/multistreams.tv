Privacy Policy

This page explains how user data is collected, used, and protected.

Last updated: June 27, 2026

At Multistreams.tv, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when using our website and services ("the Service").

Information We Collect

Analytics Data
We use analytics services, including Google Analytics 4, to collect anonymous information such as pages visited, session duration, device type, browser information, and approximate location (country or city level). This information helps us understand how our website is used and improve the user experience.

Account Information
If you create an account using supported sign-in providers, we may store your display name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier. This information is used only to provide account-related features such as saved layouts, preferences, and premium access.

Payment Information
Premium subscriptions are securely processed by trusted third-party payment providers. Multistreams.tv does not store your credit card or payment information on our servers.

Cookies

Multistreams.tv uses cookies and similar technologies to provide essential website functionality, maintain login sessions, remember preferences, and analyze website traffic. Embedded streaming platforms such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Rumble may also place cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Multistreams.tv service.
  • Save your layouts, preferences, and account settings.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Analyze anonymous usage trends.
  • Process premium subscriptions and account management.
  • Respond to support requests and inquiries.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymous or aggregated data that cannot reasonably identify an individual user. Information may also be shared when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the Service.

Third-Party Services

Multistreams.tv integrates with third-party streaming platforms including YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Rumble using their official embed technologies. When viewing embedded streams, those platforms may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party service they choose to access.

Data Retention

Analytics information is retained according to the retention policies of the analytics provider. Account information is retained while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continually work to safeguard the information entrusted to us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes have been published constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact page or by emailing support@multistreams.tv.
