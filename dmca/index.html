DMCA Policy

Use this page for copyright complaint procedures and takedown contact details.

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Multistreams.tv respects the intellectual property rights of creators, copyright holders, and third parties. We are committed to complying with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") and will promptly investigate and respond to valid copyright infringement notices.

Embedded Content

Multistreams.tv does not host, upload, store, or distribute any video or audio content on its own servers. All live streams displayed on our platform are embedded directly from publicly available streaming services, including YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Rumble, using their official embedding technologies.

All copyrights remain the property of their respective content creators, publishers, and streaming platforms. Embedding content through official platform tools is functionally equivalent to linking to content that is already publicly available on the originating platform.

Submitting a DMCA Notice

If you believe that copyrighted material displayed through Multistreams.tv infringes your rights, please submit a written DMCA notice containing the following information:

  • Your full legal name and contact information (including a valid email address).
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL on Multistreams.tv where the embedded content appears.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Review & Removal Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will review the request and take appropriate action where necessary. This may include removing the embedded content or disabling access to the affected page.

Because Multistreams.tv only embeds content from third-party platforms and does not host any media files, copyright owners may also wish to submit a removal request directly to the original hosting platform (such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, or Rumble), where the content is stored.

Counter Notification

If you believe that content was removed or disabled in error, you may submit a counter-notification that includes your contact information, identification of the removed content, and a statement made under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.

Repeat Infringers

Where appropriate, Multistreams.tv reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts or access for users who are determined to be repeat copyright infringers or who repeatedly violate the intellectual property rights of others.

Contact

To submit a DMCA notice or if you have questions regarding this policy, please contact us through our Contact page or by emailing support@multistreams.tv.

We aim to review and respond to all legitimate copyright requests within a reasonable timeframe.
