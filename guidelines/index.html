Community Guidelines

Use this page to understand the rules and expectations for using our platform.

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Multistreams.tv is built to make watching multiple live streams simple, enjoyable, and respectful for everyone. These Community Guidelines outline the standards we expect from all users. By using Multistreams.tv, you agree to follow these guidelines in addition to our Terms of Service.

Respect Others

Treat fellow users, streamers, creators, and platform communities with respect. Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, impersonation, or abusive behavior is not permitted on Multistreams.tv.

Respect Content Creators

Multistreams.tv exists to help viewers discover and watch content from creators across supported streaming platforms. Please respect creators' rights and support them through their official channels whenever possible.

  • Do not attempt to bypass advertisements or monetization systems.
  • Do not redistribute or re-stream content without permission.
  • Respect each platform's rules regarding embedded content.

Lawful Use

You may only use Multistreams.tv for lawful purposes. Activities that violate applicable laws or infringe upon the rights of others are strictly prohibited.

Account Conduct

If you create an account, you are responsible for maintaining its security and for all activity that occurs under your account.

  • Do not share or sell accounts.
  • Do not attempt to access accounts belonging to others.
  • Do not use automated tools, bots, or scripts to abuse the Service.

Platform Integrity

Help us maintain a reliable experience for everyone. The following behavior is prohibited:

  • Attempting to disrupt or overload the Service.
  • Exploiting bugs, vulnerabilities, or security issues.
  • Circumventing feature limitations or subscription requirements.
  • Using the Service in a way that negatively impacts other users.

Third-Party Platforms

Multistreams.tv embeds content from third-party services such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Rumble. When viewing content through Multistreams.tv, you are also expected to comply with the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines of those respective platforms.

Reporting Issues

If you discover abuse, security concerns, copyright issues, or behavior that violates these Guidelines, please report it through our Contact page or by emailing support@multistreams.tv. We review reports in good faith and take appropriate action when necessary.

Enforcement

To protect the Multistreams.tv community, we may investigate reports and take action against accounts or users who violate these Guidelines. Actions may include warnings, removal of saved content, temporary restrictions, suspension, or permanent termination of access depending on the severity or frequency of violations.

Changes to These Guidelines

These Community Guidelines may be updated periodically to reflect changes to our Service, supported platforms, or legal requirements. Continued use of Multistreams.tv after updates have been published constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.

Our Commitment

Our goal is to provide a fast, reliable, and creator-friendly multistream experience while respecting the communities and platforms that make live streaming possible. We appreciate every user who helps keep Multistreams.tv welcoming, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone.
